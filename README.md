# Revenue Systems

### Marketing, growth and the systems that turn demand into revenue

**By [Dev Das](https://dev-das.com/)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/HackrLife) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/devdulal/)

Revenue Systems is my evolving collection of papers, frameworks and practical operating approaches across marketing, product-led growth, demand generation, SDR and BDR operations, inside sales, enterprise selling and revenue enablement.

The work draws on my experience building marketing and growth functions across Google, Adobe, SAP, LinkedIn and IBM, alongside hands-on software development and automation.

## Start here

### [Building the Revenue System](papers/building-the-revenue-system.md)

A framework for connecting marketing strategy, product adoption, inside sales and enterprise growth.

The paper covers:

- **BANT and MEDDPICC:** choosing qualification depth to match the opportunity.
- **Demand and product signals:** connecting account evidence with useful sales action.
- **SDR-to-AE handoffs:** transferring buyer context and recording qualification gaps.
- **Seller ramp and manager coaching:** building capability around measurable productivity.
- **Customer value and expansion:** carrying the business case through implementation and adoption.
- **Automation and measurement:** supporting commercial judgement with workflows and clear metrics.

Includes **four editable Mermaid schematics**, a worked opportunity example and a phased implementation approach.

## The scope of this collection

| Area | Questions I explore |
|---|---|
| Marketing and demand | Where should investment go, and how does it create commercially valuable demand? |
| Product-led growth | How do activation, adoption and usage connect to conversion and expansion? |
| SDR and BDR operations | How do targeting, outreach, qualification and handoffs work together? |
| Inside sales and enterprise selling | What improves deal quality, velocity, win rate and productivity? |
| Enablement and revenue operations | Which playbooks, coaching practices, systems and metrics support execution? |
| Automation and AI | Which tasks can be automated, and where must people retain judgement? |

The collection starts with the paper above. Further papers, playbooks and implementation examples will be added as the work develops.
